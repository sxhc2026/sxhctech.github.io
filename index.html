热安全 · Thermal Safety

Thermal Safety

双 NTC 温度传感器（加热水温 + 出水温度）+ 温控器双重过热保护。电池模组与加热系统物理隔离，无水状态下机器不加热。

Dual NTC sensors (heating water + outlet water) + thermostat for dual overheat protection. Battery module physically isolated from heating system. No water = no heating.

💧

水路安全 · Waterway Safety

Waterway Safety

电磁阀控制水路循环。密封水箱盖 + 泄压阀防止倾倒漏水。喷水口防溢水设计。最短水路结构降低热水滞留风险。

Solenoid valve controls water circulation. Sealed tank lid + pressure relief valve prevent leakage. Anti-overflow nozzle. Shortest waterway design minimizes hot water stagnation risk.

📡

环境检测 · Environment Detection

Environment Detection

液位传感器检测水箱水位，缺水自动停机。红外传感器检测毛巾仓和托盘状态，异常自动关机保护。

Water level sensor detects tank level, auto-shutdown when dry. IR sensors monitor towel chamber and tray status, auto power-off on anomaly.

🛡️

全球认证 · Global Compliance

Global Compliance

CE-LVD + EMC + RoHS + REACH 全套认证。UN38.3 电池运输安全测试。UL/ETL/EAC/KC 可根据目标市场增加。
